# Customer-Segmentation-Analysis
Analyzed 18,000+ eCommerce transactions to uncover customer purchasing behaviors and retention opportunities.
Engineered RFM-based customer features and applied K-Means clustering to segment customers by lifecycle stage and revenue contribution.

Identified “High Value” customers represent 10% of the customer base and contribute 20.2% of total revenue, highlighting their significant impact on overall business performance.

Recommended strategic allocation of marketing budget toward re-engaging 10% of “At Risk” customers (approximately 831 customers). If their average spend increases to match that of “High Value” customers, the brand could potentially achieve an additional 10.21% increase in total revenue from the re-engagement campaign alone.

Using Tableau I built a Customer Health dashboard featuring a segmentation matrix and a revenue generated by segments chart.
